How the Green Home Score works

The score uses the fixed component weights specified in the problem statement. Each sub-score mapping is monotonic and visible.

Energy efficiency rating25%
Energy consumption intensity20%
Insulation15%
Heating system15%
Windows & glazing10%
Renewable energy10%
Smart energy management5%

Intensity is annual purchased electricity + gas divided by floor area. Current prototype maps 50 kWh/m² to 100 and 250 kWh/m² to 0; this benchmark assumption is documented and should be validated against suitable external benchmarks.
